About

I am a fourth-year Information Technology student at the University of Transport and Communications in Ho Chi Minh City. My work so far has one shape: a small business is doing something by hand every day, and I replace it with a program that runs without being watched.

That started with a shop's sales book, became an Express API and a point-of-sale screen, then a Node service that captures TikTok orders and answers messages on Zalo, then a Python tool that turns the fingerprint machine's export into a payroll sheet. None of it was coursework. All of it had a real user who would notice the next morning if it broke, which is the part that taught me the most.

I am now moving deliberately toward Python backend work — FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, tests — because that is where I want to spend the next few years. I am looking for an intern or fresher role in Ho Chi Minh City where I can read a real codebase and be corrected by people who have shipped more than I have.

How I work

01

Read the real file before writing the parser

Every bug I have lost a day to came from assuming the input was cleaner than it is. So the first job is always to look at the actual export, the actual order, the actual device.

02

Leave it runnable by someone who is not me

An .exe, a compose file, a README with the exact command. A tool that needs me to explain it is not finished.
