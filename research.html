Express first, because it was the shortest path to the shop

Both web projects started as a problem with a deadline, not as an architecture exercise: a shop needed to stop writing orders on paper. Express gave me routes in an afternoon. Data & tooling · 2024 – now

Python where the data is a spreadsheet somebody made by hand

Real small businesses live in Excel, and the exports are hostile: merged headers, dates as text, shifts crossing midnight. The payroll tool earns its keep by being honest about that — it computes what it can, then writes a separate report naming the days it refuses to guess. Packaging with PyInstaller matters as much as the logic; if the user has to open a terminal, the tool is dead.
